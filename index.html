Tips and tricks

Start

  • Find a set: type a few words, like copic 72 or posca 5m. Press Enter to take the first match.
  • Or choose Medium, then Brand, then Set. The sheet fills with the set's color codes, in number order.
  • Leave Medium on None for a blank grid, then type how many swatches you need.
  • Typing a new total switches the set off, so the sheet goes blank.

Set options

  • Leave color codes blank keeps the set's size but prints empty labels, for writing your own.
  • Ohuhu markers can use their legacy (old) codes, if your pens are older.
  • Sets made of smaller sets (like Karin) get a header with each set's name.

Layout and pages

  • Auto uses the fewest pages that still print at a good size. Untick it to choose up to 10 pages.
  • Pick a layout lists ways to split your swatches into groups. The best fit is first.
  • Spare blank swatches fill out the last group, with room for new colors later.
  • Under More options: group headers, spacing, boxes per swatch, label style and where the swatches sit.

Download and print

  • The title and description print at the top of every page. The title also names the file.
  • Excel opens in Excel and Google Sheets, so you can type in it. PDF is ready to print.
  • When printing the PDF, choose Actual size (not "Fit"), and match the paper to the one in your printer.
  • The preview shows each page as it will print.

Other page types

  • Palette strips: rows of 3 to 6 colors, each with a name box and code boxes.
  • Mixing grid: write your colors down the side and across the top. Each box is where two colors meet.
  • Both are blank, so they work with any brand.

Good to know

  • Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, there's no sign-in, and no AI is used.
  • Reset (top of Layout) puts every setting back to the start: tick Confirm first. Your theme stays.
  • Most brand sets show approximate colors in the preview. Downloads stay blank for your own swatches.
  • Your Theme choice is remembered in this browser.
