# Swatch Page Maker

Make a ready-to-print swatch sheet for your art supplies, then download it as an Excel file (.xlsx) or a PDF.

**Use it here:** https://camchromatic.com/swatch-page-maker/

Your files stay on your computer. Everything runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded, there's no sign-in, and no AI is used.

## How to use it

1. Pick a **page type**: a swatch grid, palette strips or a mixing grid.
2. For a swatch grid, pick your **medium, brand and set** (for example Alcohol marker > Copic > a 72 set), or type a total number of swatches.
3. Choose a layout, paper size and the other options. The preview shows exactly what will print.
4. Click **Download .xlsx** or **Download PDF**, print it, and swatch your colors.

To run it yourself, open `index.html` in a browser. There's nothing to install or build.

## Where the color data comes from

- **Sets and color codes** were collected from the brands' own websites and color charts and from art-supply shops (for example Blick, Jerry's Artarama, Hobby Lobby and Michaels). Some sets have only one source, so check them against your own box.
- **Preview colors** (shown on screen only; downloads stay blank) are approximate. They come from color values the brands publish on their websites, or were picked from brand and shop product pictures and ink scans. Screens and real ink differ, so treat them as a rough guide.
- Brand and product names belong to their owners. This tool isn't connected to or endorsed by any of the brands.
